Traffic cameras · the state's public cameras on the map, live

Take the traffic from WSDOT's own flow sensors

The Washington State Department of Transportation measures how its highways are flowing (wide open, moderate, heavy, stop and go), each direction on its own, about every 90 seconds, and gives the readings to anyone with a free access code (wsdot.wa.gov/traffic/api). Enter yours and each carriageway a camera watches carries the traffic the sensors beside that camera report for its direction. The code is kept in this browser only and is sent only to WSDOT, whose answer the page runs as a script (that is how WSDOT serves a page that is not its own).

Each stretch of freeway and main road takes the level of the nearest camera on its own kind of road (up to about 1.3 km along the view): the day's rhythm, unless you set what the picture shows or connect the sensors. A page cannot read a picture's pixels itself (the picture server does not allow it), so counting the vehicles in the pictures is not done here. Pictures: Washington State Department of Transportation.
