# The law book

Port Alder's laws come from the continuum images of *Corpus Compendium on Rails* (Russell Philip Smithson, 2026, corrected edition, 15 November 2026).
Of its 4,805 entries, 4,294 have a continuum image with a faithful linear form ("Exact linear", with any qualifier). The city implements 103 of them, at least one from each of the corpus's 80 stations, from their equations alone. Each is cited by station and entry; no text of the corpus is reproduced.

Part 0's classes D1 Fredholm operator, D2 Sturm-Liouville, D3 Dirichlet energy, D4 Hilbert projection, D5 kernel dimension and index, D6 exact differential form, D7 bifurcation, D8 heat semigroup, D9 geodesic distance, eikonal, D10 Fokker-Planck, D11 Euler-Lagrange, KKT, D12 Fourier symbol are the families the images belong to. Obstructions (D13, D14), structural analogues and analogies, and linearised images are not used.
